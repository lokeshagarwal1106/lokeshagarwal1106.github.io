Bubble Pop

A slingshot bubble shooter puzzle where matching the right character matters as much as making the shot.

First level free. Levels after the first require a one-time Google Play purchase. The unlock includes the generated level journey and removes ads. The app is not a fully free level campaign.

View on Google Play

How to play

  1. Look at the character loaded in your slingshot and find an identical target.
  2. Drag or tap to aim. Release the shot when the matching target has a clear path.
  3. Use SWAP when the next character gives you a better opportunity.
  4. Clear the moving targets before your limited shots run out, then continue to the next wave.

This game uses exact character matches. It does not require the usual group of three same-color bubbles.

Three useful aiming habits

Watch before firing. Rotating boards and moving formations can open or close a path. Waiting for a clear angle helps you avoid wasting a shot.

Check both shots. Your current and next characters may have different targets. SWAP changes which character you fire; it does not clear the board for you.

Retry the wave. If a wave goes wrong, replay it with a different plan. Earlier waves stay completed within that run; retrying gives you another chance to manage the limited shots.

Levels, coins and the paid unlock

The first complete level is available without buying the journey. A one-time purchase unlocks the journey of 100,000 generated levels and removes ads. Generated levels vary their characters and formations; they are not presented as 100,000 hand-designed puzzles.

Coins, daily rewards and collections are in-game features. Cosmetic unlocks and virtual coins do not replace the permanent paid level unlock. Check the Google Play price shown in your currency before confirming a purchase.

Offline play and device progress

The core game works offline within your available or purchased access. Your progress is saved on the device. No game account is needed to start. Ads, new purchases and purchase restoration require an internet connection.

Offline access does not unlock paid levels. Keep your save data when updating the app; clearing app data or uninstalling may remove local progress. Google Play purchase restoration is separate from restoring a game save.

Controls and settings

Bubble Pop supports portrait and landscape play. Settings include music volume, sound effects, haptic feedback and screen shake. Scores, three-star clears, daily missions, slingshot styles and character collections give you ways to return to your available levels.
